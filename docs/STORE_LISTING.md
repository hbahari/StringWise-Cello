## Full description (2,571/4,000)
```
Learn to play the cello from the very first day. StringWise Cello is an app for complete beginners and adult learners: a 12-week course of cello lessons, a cello tuner, a metronome and bass clef sheet music that listens through your phone's microphone as you play.

12 WEEKS OF BEGINNER CELLO LESSONS
• One lesson a week, planned for about an hour of practice a day
• Start with how to sit with the cello, set the endpin and hold the bow, and finish by playing a piece from memory
• A daily practice plan for each week that you tick off as you go
• Two pieces of sheet music every week, with finger numbers above each note
• A short timing lesson every week to build your sense of rhythm

CELLO TUNER
• Play an open string and the needle shows whether it is flat, sharp or in tune
• Tap C, G, D or A to hear the note each string should make
• Standard A = 440 Hz, or 441 to 443 Hz if your teacher or orchestra tunes higher

PLAY-ALONG SHEET MUSIC
• 22 easy pieces in the bass clef, from open-string warm-ups to Ode to Joy, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star and Mary Had a Little Lamb
• A dot on the staff shows the note you are playing, so you can see when you are on the right note
• Land the right note and the music moves on to the next one
• Hear each tune first at any tempo

FINGER PLACEMENT
• A map of cello first position on all four strings, spaced like a real fingerboard
• Tap any finger to hear its note
• The live finger check listens and tells you whether your finger is in the right place, or needs to move toward the bridge or the scroll

METRONOME AND RHYTHM PRACTICE
• A metronome with tap tempo, eighth-note clicks and silent drop-out bars
• It keeps running while you use the rest of the app
• A new rhythm to clap and play every day
• A tempo ladder that saves your fastest clean tempo for each piece

RECORD AND CHECK
• Record yourself playing with the click
• Every note is marked as right, out of tune, wrong or missed, and as early, late or on the beat
• See whether you sped up or slowed down

MUSIC THEORY FOR BEGINNERS
Short, plain-language sections on reading the bass clef, notes on the staff, rhythm and time signatures, key signatures, bowing, dynamics and how the cello is tuned.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
StringWise Cello works without an internet connection and needs no account. The microphone is its only permission. Sound is analysed on your phone and never uploaded.

StringWise Cello is a practice aid for learning cello at home. A teacher can see your posture and hear your tone in ways an app cannot, so use it alongside lessons if you can.
```
